Who I am

My storyPakistan · Remote

I'm Ayesha Ramzan, an AI engineer and full-stack developer. I don't just write code; I build products people actually use.

My work spans the whole stack. On the frontend that means React, Next.js, TypeScript and clean, responsive interfaces. Under the hood it's Python with Flask and FastAPI, Node.js, PostgreSQL and Docker. Most of my energy now goes into agentic AI: assistants that understand a request, decide the next step, call tools and finish the job on their own.

I've built a WhatsApp bot that takes restaurant orders and payments, a wake-word voice assistant that controls my laptop, an auto-reply bot that answers chats in my own voice, and web apps that are live on Vercel. Each one started with a real problem, not a tech stack.

I also work as a developer at Gobind Trades on GTOS, the company's workforce platform, where I ship employee-app features that site crews use every day, covered by integration and end-to-end tests.
