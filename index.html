About me

I'm Ayesha Ramzan — an AI & full-stack developer who builds software that can talk, listen and get real work done.

I'm an AI Engineer and Full-Stack Developer. I design agentic AI systems — assistants that understand a request, decide the next step, call tools and APIs, and finish the task on their own — and wrap them in fast, clean products people enjoy using.

On the frontend I build responsive interfaces with React, Next.js and TypeScript. On the backend I work mainly in Python — Flask and FastAPI services, LLM integrations, automation and data pipelines — with Node.js, PostgreSQL and Docker when the project needs them. From idea to deployment, I handle the whole stack.
